# Product Management

*How to take a product from idea to market — and why the best product managers are not the ones with the best features, but the ones who understand the problem best.*

---

## What This Course Is About

Product Management sits at the intersection of business, technology, and design. This course covers the full lifecycle — from identifying customer problems and validating ideas, through development and positioning, to go-to-market strategy and adoption. The emphasis is on frameworks and case-based learning, not tools.

:::tip[How to read these notes]
Each session opens with a **Priority Map**. Inline boxes flag importance: **Must Know — Exam Critical** (red) is highest priority, **Important** (green) is supporting, **Good to Know** (grey) is background colour. Skim the red and green boxes first when short on time.
:::

## Course Structure

The course is taught by **Prof. Pranto Banerjee** across 10 sessions and uses a mix of lectures, five graded case studies (redBus, Zenatix/WattMan, LendenClub, Dropbox, Nexent Systems), and reading materials. The teaching method is **CCCA** — Conceptual Clarity followed by Contextual Applicability.

## Core Frameworks

- **Problem Space vs. Solution Space** — why understanding the problem matters more than building the solution
- **RICE Prioritization** and **Customer Perceived Value** — deciding what to build and what customers actually value
- **Positioning & the Buyer Utility Map** — conveying what your product does, for whom, and why it wins
- **Iteration vs. Pivot** and **Product-Market Fit** — discovering and validating fit
- **The Stage-Gate Model** — how new products are actually built
- **A/B Testing & Difference-in-Differences** — letting data decide
- **Go-to-Market, Freemium & SaaS Economics (LTV/CAC)** — taking it to market with a metric, not a hunch
- **Product Life Cycle, Rogers' Diffusion & the Chasm** — adoption and diffusion
- **Product Roadmapping (themes × releases × JTBD)** — turning a feature list into a journey

## The Five Cases

- **redBus** — the art and science of product management; funnel and conversion
- **Zenatix / WattMan** — product-market fit and the three pivots
- **LendenClub** — new product development in the digital space; A/B testing
- **Dropbox** — go-to-market strategy, freemium, and SaaS economics
- **Nexent Systems** — the product roadmap blues; adoption and diffusion
