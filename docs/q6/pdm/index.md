# Product Management

*How to take a product from idea to market — and why the best product managers are not the ones with the best features, but the ones who understand the problem best.*

---

## What This Course Is About

Product Management sits at the intersection of business, technology, and design. This course covers the full lifecycle — from identifying customer problems and validating ideas, through development and positioning, to go-to-market strategy and beyond. The emphasis is on frameworks and case-based learning, not tools.

## Course Structure

The course is taught by **Prof. Pranto Banerjee** and uses a mix of lectures, case studies (Red Bus, Zentatix, LendenClub, and others), and reading materials to build a practical understanding of what product managers actually do.

## Core Frameworks

- **Problem Space vs. Solution Space** — why understanding the problem matters more than building the solution
- **Customer Value** — B2C value elements and B2B value propositions
- **Positioning** — how to convey what your product does for the customer
- **Buyer Utility Map** — mapping utility across the buyer experience cycle
- **Product-Market Fit** — discovering and validating it
