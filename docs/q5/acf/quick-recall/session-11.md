# Session 11: Options - The Insurance Contracts of Finance

## The Story

You buy travel insurance before a trip. Most of the time you never use it — that's the point. You're not betting something will go wrong; you're just refusing to be wiped out if it does. You pay a small, known cost upfront, and in exchange your worst-case outcome is capped, while nothing limits your best case.

## The Concept

That's exactly what a financial option is. A call option lets you buy something at a fixed price later if it's worth it; a put option lets you sell at a fixed price if things go bad. Either way, your maximum loss is just the premium you paid — never more.

## Why This Matters as a Future Manager

Negotiate a client contract with an opt-out clause. Run a small pilot before a full rollout. Ask for a trial period before a full commitment. All of these are literally buying an option — paying a small cost to cap your downside while keeping the upside open.

## The One-Liner

*"You don't buy insurance because you're sure something bad will happen — you buy it because you can't afford it if it does."*
