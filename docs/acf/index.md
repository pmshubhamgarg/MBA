# Advanced Corporate Finance (ACF)

Building on the foundations of Intro to Corporate Finance — integrating investment, financing, and distribution decisions into a unified value-creation framework.

**Textbook:** Same as ICF (continued)

**Evaluation:**
- Quizzes (50%) — unannounced, in-class, best 10 of ~13, 5 marks each
- Class Participation (20%) — quality over quantity, includes responses and good questions
- End-term Exam (30%)
