# Fundamentals of AI

*How artificial intelligence actually works, why it is different from every wave of technology before it, and what a business leader needs to know to make good decisions with it.*

---

## What This Course Is About

Fundamentals of AI (FAI) is a 2-credit core course in the Executive MBA program at IIM Udaipur, taught over 10 online sessions by **Prof. Probal Mojumder**. It is designed for senior managers who need to move beyond the buzzwords — to understand what AI can and cannot do, how machine learning actually works under the hood, and how to lead an organization that increasingly runs on data-driven models.

The course does not require prior programming knowledge. Its aim is not to turn managers into ML engineers, but to equip them to make informed decisions about where, when, and how to apply AI in a business — and to think clearly about the ethical and organizational questions that follow.

:::tip[How to read these notes]
Each session opens with a **Priority Map**. Inline boxes flag importance: **Must Know — Exam Critical** (red) is highest priority, **Important** (green) is supporting, **Good to Know** (grey) is background color. Skim the red and green boxes first when short on time.
:::

## Course Philosophy

The professor's core thesis: AI is not just a new tool in the toolbox. It is a **decisive technology** that changes work dynamics inside organizations, reshapes stakeholder value, and rewrites customer experience. The gap between high-performing and low-performing companies over the next decade will be defined by how well they harness AI.

But AI is also not magic. It is math, data, and disciplined engineering. Managers who understand the mechanics — even at an intuitive level — make dramatically better decisions than those who only understand the marketing.

## Core Themes

- **Taxonomy of AI** — rule-based systems vs neural networks, narrow vs general AI, discriminative vs generative
- **How ML actually works** — neurons, weights, cost functions, gradient descent, backpropagation
- **Computer vision** — CNNs, convolution, and where they are transforming business
- **Natural language processing** — from bag-of-words to RNNs to transformers and LLMs
- **The 3 horizons of AI adoption** — from task automation to organizational capability
- **Ethical AI** — bias, fairness, privacy, accountability, and responsible deployment
- **Prompt engineering** — practical skills for working with modern LLMs
