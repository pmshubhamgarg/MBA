# Marketing in Digital World

*Why "digital" isn't just a channel — it's a fundamentally different world with different rules for creating, delivering, and capturing value.*

---

## What This Course Is About

This course draws a sharp line between **"Digital Marketing"** (using digital tools to do traditional marketing) and **"Marketing in the Digital World"** (rethinking marketing because the world itself has changed). The digital world isn't just a new place to run ads — it rewrites the economics of production, distribution, communication, and customer relationships.

## Course Structure

| Module | Sessions | Focus |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Module 1 — Conceptual Foundations** | 1–14 | How marketing strategy changes when infrastructure is rentable, segments shrink to micro-segments, and data replaces intuition |
| **Module 2 — AI Tools & Practice** | 15–20 | Hands-on with AI-powered marketing tools, analytics, and automation |

## Core Frameworks

- **Scaled vs. Unscaled Economy** — why mass production logic breaks in a digital world
- **Own vs. Rent** — what to build yourself vs. what to borrow from platforms
- **Value Proposition Shift** — from one-size-fits-all to personalized-for-niche
- **Micro-Segmentation** — from demographic buckets to behavioral clusters
- **Personas** — turning data into fictional people you can design for
