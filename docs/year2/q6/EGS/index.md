# Environment, Governance & Sustainability (EGS)

*How sustainability moves from CSR reporting to core business strategy, decision-making, and execution.*

---

## What This Course Is About

EGS focuses on one practical question: **how do companies make sustainability a real strategy decision, not a presentation slide?**

From the sessions processed so far, the course emphasizes:

- sustainability as a **CEO/board agenda**, not only a sustainability-team function
- the shift from broad “ESG talk” to **focused material priorities**
- balancing **financial impact** and **societal impact** (double materiality)
- selecting fewer, high-impact actions using practical lenses
- institutionalizing sustainability through strategy, operations, and governance
