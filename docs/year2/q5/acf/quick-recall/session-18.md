# Session 18: Corporate Valuation Bridge and the Interco Takeover Battle

## The Story

A rival company makes an offer to take over yours — and calls it "generous." At first glance, the number looks fair. But when you rebuild the valuation yourself, you discover the offer quietly used cherry-picked comparisons and a subtly tilted discount rate — both designed to make an underpriced offer look reasonable. Once you expose that, the board rejects it and the price collapses on its own.

## The Concept

Learn to build (and unbuild) a company valuation yourself — from cash flow, to enterprise value, to what it's actually worth per share — rather than trusting a number someone else built for you.

## Why This Matters as a Future Manager

Someone, someday, will hand you a number "designed to look fair" while quietly favoring them — in an acquisition offer, a salary negotiation, a partnership split. The only real protection is knowing how to rebuild that number yourself instead of accepting it at face value.

## The One-Liner

*"Learn to build the valuation yourself, because someone will eventually hand you one designed to look fair while quietly favoring them."*
