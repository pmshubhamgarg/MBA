# Session 15: International Capital Budgeting — Global CAPM and Country Risk Premium

## The Story

Your company wants to invest in a country where the target isn't listed anywhere, so there's no clean way to measure "how risky is this specific investment." You build a stand-in estimate using comparable listed companies elsewhere — and then add something extra on top: a premium for the simple fact that this country could change its rules, restrict currency movement, or have a political shock tomorrow that a stable market wouldn't.

## The Concept

That's Global CAPM (borrowing a risk estimate from comparable companies when you have no local one) plus Country Risk Premium (pricing in the risk of the ground itself shifting).

## Why This Matters as a Future Manager

Even outside pure finance, any manager assessing expansion into an unfamiliar market, region, or segment with no track record needs this exact instinct: borrow a reasonable estimate from something comparable, and never forget to price in the risk that's specific to *where* you're going, not just *what* you're doing.

## The One-Liner

*"When there's no track record to measure against, borrow one from something comparable — and never forget to price in the risk of the ground shifting beneath you."*
